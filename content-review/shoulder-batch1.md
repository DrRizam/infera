**Stem:** Your pretest probability of subacromial pathology is about 50%. The Park cluster comes back fully positive (LR+ ≈ 10). Roughly what is the post-test probability?

**Stem:** What is the defining strength of the Patient-Specific Functional Scale (PSFS) — and its corresponding limitation?
