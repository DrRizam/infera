# Installing Clinician on your phone

Thanks for testing this. It takes about a minute, and there's no app store, no
account, and no sign-up — you open a link and add it to your home screen.

**The link:** https://whimsical-taiyaki-e74b8b.netlify.app/

---

## iPhone / iPad (Safari)

1. Open the link **in Safari**. (Chrome on iPhone can't install apps — if you
   tapped the link from a message and it opened somewhere else, copy the URL
   into Safari.)
2. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the
   bottom of the screen.
3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top right.

You'll now have a Clinician icon on your home screen. Open it from there, not
from Safari — it runs full screen and works offline that way.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. You may see an **Install app** prompt at the bottom — tap it and you're done.
3. If you don't see the prompt: tap the **⋮** menu (top right) and choose
   **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

---

## How to use it

Open it once a day and tap **Start**. A session is about 5–10 minutes.

- When you answer correctly, you're asked how it felt: **Shaky**, **Got it**, or
  **Knew it cold**. Answer honestly — that's what decides when the item comes
  back. Guessing "Knew it cold" only cheats yourself out of the review.
- Wrong answers come back sooner. That's the system working, not a punishment.
- **Missing a day is fine.** Practising 7 days earns you a banked rest day, and
  a missed day quietly spends that instead of your streak. No lives, no hearts,
  nothing to buy.

## Please flag bad content

This matters more than anything else you can do for me.

The clinical content is drafted from published sources and reviewed, but some
of it is still marked **⚠️ unverified** in the app — you'll see the badge and
the citation under each answer. If something looks wrong, outdated, badly
worded, or just off:

**Tap "⚑ Something wrong with this item?" at the bottom of the question**, type
what's wrong, and send. It's saved on your phone — nothing is transmitted.

## Sending me your feedback

At the end of the two weeks (or any time):

1. Go to the **Stats** tab.
2. Tap **⬇️ Back up my progress**.
3. Send me the file that downloads.

That file has your progress and every item you flagged. It's also your own
backup — worth doing occasionally regardless, since everything is stored only
on your phone.

---

## Things worth knowing

- **Your data never leaves your phone.** No accounts, no analytics, no server.
  The flip side: if you delete the app or clear your browser data, your progress
  is gone unless you've backed it up.
- **It works offline** once installed — on the train, in a basement clinic,
  anywhere.
- **It updates itself.** When I push a fix, you get it next time you open it.
- **This is a study aid, not a diagnostic tool**, and not medical advice.

Questions, or something broken? Just message me.
