Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy applies to the Infera Premium subscription. Payments and refunds are handled by our merchant of record, Paddle.

1. Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel Infera Premium at any time from the customer portal — reachable from Settings in the app, or from the management link in your Paddle receipt. When you cancel:

  • your subscription stops renewing;
  • you keep Premium access until the end of the period you have already paid for;
  • no further charges are made.

2. 14-day refund on your first purchase

If you are unhappy with Premium, you can request a full refund within 14 days of your first Premium payment, for any reason. Contact us at rizamshaar2014@gmail.com or use the help link on your Paddle receipt. We will approve first-purchase refund requests made within this window.

3. After the first 14 days

Outside the 14-day window, subscription payments are generally non-refundable, and we do not provide pro-rata refunds for the unused part of a billing period. Instead, cancel before your next renewal date so you are not charged again — you will keep access until the period ends.

We may still issue a refund at our discretion in specific circumstances, for example a duplicate charge, a clear billing error, or a prolonged failure of the Service that we could not resolve.

4. Renewals

Because subscriptions renew automatically and we send no separate reminder for every renewal, please cancel in good time if you do not want to be charged again. A renewal charge on its own is not treated as a first-purchase refund.

5. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy affects any non-waivable rights you have under the consumer-protection law of your country of residence. Where such law gives you a stronger right to cancel or to a refund, that right applies.

6. How to request a refund

  1. Email rizamshaar2014@gmail.com from the address on your account, or use the "Need help?" / support link on your Paddle receipt.
  2. Include your account email and, if you have it, the Paddle order or transaction reference.
  3. Approved refunds are issued by Paddle to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.

7. Chargebacks

If you believe you have been charged in error, please contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a bank dispute. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us may result in your account being suspended pending resolution.
