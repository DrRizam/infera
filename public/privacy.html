Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy is written by the operator of Infera and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It describes, in plain terms, what data Infera handles and why.

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Infera (the "Operator", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Infera website and apps (the "Service").

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your email address (for sign-in) and, optionally, a display name and profile details you choose to add (such as role or country).
  • Learning data: your activity in the app — case answers, accuracy and reasoning-breakdown scores, XP, levels, streaks, competency estimates, and which cases and drills you have completed.
  • Technical data: basic information your device and browser send automatically (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed), used for security and to keep the Service running.
  • Support data: anything you send us by email or through feedback forms.

2. What we do not collect

  • No real patient data. Every case in Infera is fictional. Nothing you enter describes an actual patient, and you must not submit information that identifies a real person.
  • No card details. Payments are handled entirely by Paddle (see below); we never see or store your card or bank information.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising profiling.

3. How we use your data

We use the data above only to operate the Service:

  • to create and secure your account and authenticate you;
  • to track your progress, schedule reviews, and show you your own statistics and weak spots;
  • to provide the free and Premium tiers and enforce usage limits;
  • to respond to support requests;
  • to detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and security issues;
  • to understand aggregate, non-identifying usage so we can improve the app.

4. Payments

Our reseller and merchant of record, Paddle, processes all payments and manages subscriptions, invoices, and refunds. When you buy Premium, Paddle collects the information it needs to complete the transaction (for example, name, billing address, payment method, and tax location) under Paddle's own privacy policy. We receive from Paddle only the information needed to activate and manage your subscription (such as your subscription status and a customer identifier).

5. Service providers

We rely on a small number of processors who handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and hosting of your account and learning data.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting, content delivery, and security.
  • Paddle — payments and subscription management.
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Google AdMob — if you use the mobile app on the free tier, ads may be served by AdMob, which may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. The web app does not show ads.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser storage that are necessary for the Service to work — for example, to keep you signed in and to remember lightweight preferences. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on the website.

7. Data retention

We keep your account and learning data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account (Profile → Delete account), your data is permanently erased immediately. Payment and tax records held by Paddle are retained by Paddle as required by law.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can exercise the main rights yourself in the app (view your data on your Profile; delete everything from Profile → Delete account), or contact us at the address below. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers are covered by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

Infera is intended for adults (18+ or the age of majority where you live) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the date above and, where appropriate, notified to you.
